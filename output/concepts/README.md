# Stack City — six city directions

One-sheet visual comparison requested by Art on 2026-11-03. Six treatments share a city layout and elevated gameplay camera: NFL Clash; Last Arcade / Project Ocean; FarmVille 2; mature ink sketch; Into the Spider-Verse; The Sims / Express Yourself.

Final deliverable: `city-style-comparison-v02.png`. These are AI-generated art-direction concepts, not screenshots of implemented gameplay. Generation used the built-in image tool, followed by a targeted edit to emphasize FarmVille 2 bevels and Last Arcade / Project Ocean faceting. The two exact prompts are saved alongside the sheet.

## Reference basis

- NFL Clash: inspected Art's local `nflc_AD_stadiums.png`, corresponding to the stadium work on [Art Klein's portfolio](https://www.artkleinart.com/art-direction.html). The target was its environment-rendering style, applied to a neighborhood rather than a stadium.
- Last Arcade: inspected Art's local `lastarcade_concept_env.png`, `lastarcade_concept_inGame.png`, and art-design document under `2026Aug/LastArcade/Pre-production`. Art confirmed this is his game on utilityinfielder.com. Used as a style reference, not as the city layout or gameplay specification.
- Project Ocean: inspected Art's local `public-site/assets/project-ocean-v6.webp` cast reference. Used for faceted forms and mature angular proportions.
- FarmVille 2: chunky bevels and edge highlights were explicitly requested. [Production artist reference](https://frankvanderwel.artstation.com/projects/y4ZqJ) describes hand-painted low-poly assets and colored ambient occlusion; this informed the brief.
- Mature ink sketch and Into the Spider-Verse: separate illustration treatments as requested, with quiet paper/ink washes in one and graphic comic print effects in the other.
- The Sims / Express Yourself: interpreted the requested feminine-forward color direction as ivory, dusty rose, lilac, mint, peach and plum. The exact Roblox title could not be confidently identified, so this is a palette interpretation, not a verified color match to that game.

No external reference assets are redistributed in this folder. Images are concepts for review; the production Three.js materials, geometry and lighting remain unchanged.
