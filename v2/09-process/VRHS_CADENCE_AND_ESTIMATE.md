# VR High School cadence and Stack City V2 estimate

Prepared 2026-11-05. This is a planning estimate, not a time sheet or a deadline.

## Owner availability

Art reports 4–6 focused hours on weekdays and 1–2 on weekends. Assuming the weekend amount is per day:

- Minimum weekly availability: 5 × 4 + 2 × 1 = **22 hours**.
- Maximum: 5 × 6 + 2 × 2 = **34 hours**.
- Midpoint planning example: 5 × 5 + 2 × 1.5 = **28 hours/week**.

If 1–2 means the entire weekend, use 21–32 hours/week instead. The broad calendar recommendation remains about 2–4 weeks.

## What can and cannot be inferred

The pattern supports rapid assembly followed by several distinct refinement passes. It does not establish that every intervening hour was active work, that each commit is one session, or that an AI-assisted artifact took the same effort as a handmade drawing.

The history covers a recent refresh of an older project with existing source material. It is not the complete project lifetime. Local work, uncommitted changes, work in another conversation, research, and drawing away from the computer are not fully visible.

There is no measured manual-drawing pace here. The handmade allowance below is an explicit scope estimate. Repository and artifact timestamps cannot isolate Art's time from AI/tool activity.

## Calendar interpretation

- At 28 hours/week: **about 2–3 weeks** of focused effort.
- At the full availability extremes: 55/34 = 1.6 weeks; 85/22 = 3.9 weeks.
- Practical recommendation: **target three weeks; allow two to four weeks** for variation and review.

A rough first pass could follow the observed rapid VRHS cadence, but that should not be confused with finishing a robust three-direction case study.

## Suggested sequence

**Week 1:** lock the learning question, sketch all three directions, review major silhouettes, establish an environment/object study and a rough key screen per direction.

**Week 2:** refine the three directions, choose the deeper interaction, build the native UI system and prototype, draft the guide around real evidence.

**Week 3:** finish the selected handmade studies, test/readjust the interaction, document extension to another asset, edit the guide/pitch, export, rehearse.

Use the weekend for a small bounded drawing, annotation, or review task. Avoid relying on a long weekend production sprint given the reported availability.

## What changes the estimate

More full scene production, three deep prototypes, a new website, an app implementation, detailed 3D modeling, or additional directions require a separate estimate. New WorldFarm feature development is not included beyond a small adaptation allowance taken from the environment/contingency budget.

Use one actual session in each of drawing, environment development, and Figma to recalibrate. If manual studies take longer than the allowance, reduce quantity or extend the calendar rather than remove evidence of original work.
