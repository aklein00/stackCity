# Syd Mead Retro-Future

## Identity to test

Functional product families, engineered volumes, service layers, modular growth, and purposeful signal color.

## References

Mead's Future Urban Architecture and the official-store Gyro Pods illustration; Satisfactory for functional grouping; ASTRONEER for broad color and equipment readability; FTL for spatial systems and allocation consequences.

## First work

Cabin workspace → service-bay garage → commercial pod.
Props: delivery pod, cargo crate, fabrication bench, solar canopy, vendor terminal, upgrade connector.
UI: inspect → compare operating plans → preview reserve/capacity → confirm → see changed module.

## Handmade proof

Pod silhouettes, connection/interface sketches, a service-bay composition, and an operating-plan screen.

## Guardrails

Keep the direction optimistic and legible. Avoid copying a franchise, adding meaningless machine detail, or importing a cockpit full of controls. Teen appeal is a hypothesis about experimentation and customization.
