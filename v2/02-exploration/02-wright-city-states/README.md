# Wright-inspired City-States

## Identity to test

Horizontal planes, terraces, repeated civic bays, bounded accents, useful objects, and city identity expressed through architecture.

## References

Wright's Fallingwater, Unity Temple, and Robie House art glass; the reconstructed Stoa of Attalos; a Panathenaic prize amphora. Neiman supplies celebration/color relationships; Leyendecker supplies deliberate painted planes. UI precedents: ISLANDERS and Dorfromantik.

## First work

Courtyard workshop → covered market → Games pavilion.
Props: stall, olive-oil jar, prize vessel, shade screen, lantern, supply cart.
UI: overview → investment preview → two-city comparison → event outcome.

## Handmade proof

Terrace silhouettes, market-bay studies, a prize-vessel sheet, and city-comparison wireframes.

## Guardrails

This is an original synthesis, not historical reconstruction or a claim that Wright designed Greek buildings. Personal and business learning need experiences within the civic layer. Compare readiness, reserves, capacity, and dependencies rather than only wealth. Authored comparison cities can precede multiplayer.
