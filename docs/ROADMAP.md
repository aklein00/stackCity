## Validation plan

Review an initial path with a teen audience when available. Check comprehension of available money versus total cash, clarity of purchase consequences, ability to recover, legibility on a smaller screen, and whether a new scenario can be understood. Formal learning claims require evidence beyond a pitch demo.

## Risks and responses

- Scope expands into city builder + life sim + driving game: keep one block and one learning loop.
- Monetization undermines education: keep paid cosmetics separate from simulated finances.
- Visually inconsistent generated art: lock camera and create a reusable blockout.
- Missing brief changes direction: keep assumptions explicit and scenes modular.
- Figma prototype mistaken for runtime: document curated branches and mocked world changes.
- WorldFarm consumes the schedule: retain game-first milestone order.
